## Findings
- [MAJOR] drive.spec does not actually test the once-per-tuple / re-occurrence contract — e2e/drive.spec.ts:15-41. `drive` awaits `act` before it polls again (e2e/bots.ts:91-120). In the fixture, the act for `b` itself does all three things: it sets the root back to `a|1|narrator`, waits 150 ms and then removes the root, all before it returns. So drive never sees the returning tuple. drive's next poll finds no root and returns. Every act also changes the tuple, so no step ever leaves the state unchanged. As a result, a drive with no dedupe at all (act on every poll), or one that only dedupes against the last tuple (exactly the A→B→A failure the plan calls out), would also produce `seen` = [a1n, a2n, a2l, b] and pass. The test title says 'Wiederkehr löst nicht erneut aus', but the test does not prove it, so the DoD 'nachgewiesen per page.setContent-Fixture' is not met. The implementation looks correct on inspection (it keeps a seen-Set and passes `[...seen]` into waitForFunction). Fix: trigger the re-occurrence outside `act`, for example with an in-page setTimeout chain (b → after ~100 ms a|1|narrator → after ~300 ms remove the root), and add a case where `act` leaves the tuple unchanged for a while and drive must not call it again.
